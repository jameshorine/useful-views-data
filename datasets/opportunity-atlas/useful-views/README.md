## outcomes-long.parquet

The Opportunity Atlas's tract, county and commuting-zone outcomes in long form: one row per geography, place, outcome (household income rank or incarceration), race and gender that has an estimate, with its standard error and the number of low-income children it stands for.

- Recipe: `recipes/outcomes_long/`
- Inputs: `useful-views/tract-outcomes.parquet`, `useful-views/county-outcomes.parquet`, `useful-views/cz-outcomes.parquet`

Steps:

1. Turn each wide table's kfr and jail columns into one row per place, outcome, race and gender, keeping the place's commuting zone and the race-gender count, leave out the cells without an estimate, sort by the key, and check that every published estimate is there once. (`01_to_long.py`)
